THE CONCEPT

Why Use a Queue?

A queue is a linear data structure that follows First In, First Out (FIFO). Just like customers lining up at a bank, the first customer to join is the first customer to be served.

Enqueue

Add a new customer at the rear of the queue.

Dequeue

Serve and remove the customer at the front.

Front / Peek

See who is next without removing them.

FIFO

Preserves arrival order and promotes fairness.

Other Real World Uses

Printer jobs, customer support requests, supermarket checkout lines, ticket booking, and CPU process scheduling all use queue concepts. Some systems, such as emergency departments, need priority queues rather than simple FIFO queues.
